# Dataset InformationData Privacy NoticeThe original dataset used in this project is not included in this repository.This decision was made to protect:Patient privacyConfidential insurance informationSensitive medical dataPersonally Identifiable Information (PII)---Dataset DescriptionThe analysis was performed on an insurance claims dataset containing approximately 8,001 claim records.The dataset includes the following categories of information:Category	ExamplesPolicy Information	policy_id, policy_type, sum_insured, policy_start_datePatient Demographics	age, gender, marital_status, state, city, occupationMedical Risk Factors	bmi, tobacco_usage, has_diabetes, has_hypertension, family_history_cardiacClaim Details	claim_date, hospital_name, hospital_tier, length_of_stay, icu_daysFinancial Information	hospital_bill_amount, total_claim_amount, deductible_amount, co_pay_amount---Data ProcessingThe dataset was used for:Data Cleaning – Handling missing values, standardizing formatsFeature Engineering – Creating financial and medical risk indicatorsRisk Scoring – Developing Medical Risk Score and Overall Claim Risk ScoreAnomaly Detection – Statistical identification of unusual claims using Z-scoresDashboard Development – Building interactive Power BI reports---Data SourceThis project uses synthetic or anonymized insurance claims data.No real patient or insurance data was used in this project.---ContactFor any questions regarding the dataset or methodology, please feel free to reach out.---Last Updated: September 2025
