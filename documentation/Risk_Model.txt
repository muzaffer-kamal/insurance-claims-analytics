Risk Model DocumentationThis document describes the risk scoring framework developed for the Insurance Claims Analytics project.---ObjectiveThe objective of the risk model is to:Quantify the medical risk associated with each claimQuantify the financial risk associated with each claimCombine medical and financial risk into a single Overall Claim Risk ScoreClassify claims into risk levels for prioritization---Medical Risk ScoreComponentsThe Medical Risk Score is composed of five risk indicators:Component	DescriptionBMI Risk	Risk based on Body Mass Index classificationTobacco Risk	Risk based on tobacco usageChronic Risk	Risk based on chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension)Family History Risk	Risk based on family history of cardiac conditionsScoringEach component contributes to the Medical Risk Score.The Medical Risk Score ranges from 0 to 7.A higher score indicates higher medical risk.NormalizationThe Medical Risk Score is normalized to a 0–1 scale:`Medical_Risk_Normalized = Medical_Risk_Score / 7`---Financial Risk FeaturesThe following financial features were engineered for the risk model:Feature	DescriptionClaim Ratio	Claim Value ÷ Sum InsuredCost Per Day	Hospital Bill ÷ Length of StayClaim Delay	Claim Date - Policy Start DateOut of Pocket	Deductible + Co-pay + Non-payable ItemsIncome-to-Claim Risk	Claim Value ÷ Annual Income---NormalizationEach financial feature was normalized using Min-Max Normalization:`Normalized Value = (Value - Min) / (Max - Min)`This ensures all features are on a comparable 0–1 scale.Normalized FeaturesFeature	Normalized FieldClaim Ratio	Claim_Ratio_NormalizedCost Per Day	Cost_Per_Day_NormalizedClaim Delay	Claim_Delay_NormalizedOut of Pocket	Out_of_Pocket_NormalizedIncome-to-Claim Risk	Income_to_Claim_Risk_Normalized---Overall Claim Risk ScoreWeighting FrameworkThe Overall Claim Risk Score is calculated using weighted sum of six normalized features:Risk Factor	WeightMedical Risk	30%Claim Ratio	25%Cost Per Day	20%Claim Delay	10%Out of Pocket	10%Income-to-Claim Risk	5%Total	100%Formula`Overall_Claim_Risk_Score = (Medical_Risk_Normalized × 0.30) + (Claim_Ratio_Normalized × 0.25) + (Cost_Per_Day_Normalized × 0.20) + (Claim_Delay_Normalized × 0.10) + (Out_of_Pocket_Normalized × 0.10) + (Income_to_Claim_Risk_Normalized × 0.05)`RangeThe Overall Claim Risk Score ranges from 0 to 1.A higher score indicates higher overall risk.---Risk ClassificationClaims are classified into risk levels based on the Overall Claim Risk Score:Score Range	Risk LevelLess than 0.30	Low Risk0.30 to less than 0.60	Medium Risk0.60 to less than 0.80	High Risk0.80 and above	Potential Anomaly---InterpretationLow Risk (Score < 0.30)Claims with low medical and financial riskRoutine claims that may not require additional reviewMedium Risk (Score 0.30–0.60)Claims with moderate medical or financial riskMay benefit from periodic reviewHigh Risk (Score 0.60–0.80)Claims with significant medical or financial riskShould be reviewed for potential issuesPotential Anomaly (Score ≥ 0.80)Claims with very high risk scoresMay require additional investigation---Important NoteThe risk model is designed for analytical prioritization.It does not indicate confirmed fraud.Claims identified as High Risk or Potential Anomaly should be reviewed by subject matter experts.---Model ValidationThe risk model was validated through:Distribution analysis of risk scoresReview of risk level distributionVerification of normalization rangesTesting of Z-score calculations---LimitationsThe model is based on available data fieldsAdditional risk factors could improve predictive powerThe model is designed for insurance claims and may not generalize to other domainsThe model should be periodically reviewed and updated---SummaryThe risk model provides a structured framework for:Quantifying medical riskQuantifying financial riskCombining risks into a single scorePrioritizing claims for reviewThis framework enables data-driven risk assessment and supports efficient claims review processes.---Last Updated: September 2025
