# Insurance Claims Analytics## Project OverviewThis project presents an end-to-end insurance claims analytics solution developed using Microsoft Power BI.The objective is to analyze medical and financial insurance claims, identify key risk drivers, develop a claim risk scoring framework, and detect statistically unusual claims that may require further investigation.The project combines data cleaning, feature engineering, risk scoring, exploratory data analysis, financial analysis, and statistical anomaly detection into an interactive Power BI dashboard.---## Business ProblemInsurance organizations process large volumes of healthcare claims and need analytical tools to identify:- High-risk claims- Unusual financial patterns- Excessive claim ratios- Unusually high daily treatment costs- Delayed claims- High out-of-pocket expenses- Claims requiring further investigationThis project addresses these needs through a structured analytical framework.---## Project ObjectivesThe main objectives of this project are to:1. Clean and standardize insurance claims data.2. Engineer medical and financial risk features.3. Develop a Medical Risk Score.4. Develop an Overall Claim Risk Score.5. Classify claims into risk levels.6. Perform financial risk analysis.7. Detect statistical anomalies using Z-scores.8. Identify claims that may require further investigation.9. Present the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard.---## DatasetThe project uses an insurance claims dataset containing approximately 8,001 claim records.The dataset includes medical, financial, policy, hospital, and demographic attributes.Examples include:- Policy information- Patient demographic attributes- Medical risk factors- Hospital information- Claim amounts- Hospitalization duration- Insurance coverage- Deductibles- Co-payments- Non-payable expensesThe original dataset is not included in this repository where privacy or confidentiality restrictions apply.---## Data PreparationThe data preparation process included:- Data type correction- Standardization of categorical values- Handling inconsistent entries- Financial field validation- Feature creation- Risk categorization- Derived financial metricsPower Query in Power BI was used extensively during the data preparation stage.---## Feature EngineeringSeveral analytical features were created.### Financial Features- Claim Ratio- Cost Per Day- Claim Delay- Bill Difference- Out of Pocket Expense- Income to Claim Ratio### Medical Features- BMI Group- BMI Risk- Tobacco Risk- Chronic Risk- Family History Risk- Medical Risk Score---## Risk Scoring FrameworkA Medical Risk Score was developed from selected medical risk factors.The project then combines medical and financial indicators into an Overall Claim Risk Score.The weighting framework is:| Risk Factor | Weight ||---|---:|| Medical Risk | 30% || Claim Ratio | 25% || Cost Per Day | 20% || Claim Delay | 10% || Out of Pocket | 10% || Income-to-Claim Risk | 5% || **Total** | **100%** |The Overall Claim Risk Score is used to rank claims according to their relative medical and financial risk.---## Risk ClassificationClaims are classified according to their Overall Claim Risk Score.The current classification framework includes:- Low Risk- Medium Risk- High Risk- Potential AnomalyThe risk classification is intended for analytical prioritization and does not represent confirmed fraud.---## Anomaly DetectionA separate statistical anomaly detection layer was developed.Four Z-score features are used:- Claim Ratio Z-score- Cost Per Day Z-score- Claim Delay Z-score- Total Claim Amount Z-scoreA value greater than or equal to +3 standard deviations is treated as a strong statistical signal.An `Anomaly_Count` field counts the number of indicators exceeding this threshold.Claims with two or more such signals are classified as:`Potential Anomaly`This classification identifies claims that may warrant further investigation. It does not indicate confirmed fraud.---## Dashboard Pages### 1.
